Courses

Not sure where to start? In the free trial lesson I listen to your reading and suggest the right course.

Noorani Qaida

Arabic letters, sounds and joining. For beginners and young children.

Quran reading

Read the Quran from the mushaf, smoothly and correctly.

Tajweed

The rules of pronunciation and recitation, practised aloud in every lesson.

Hifz (memorization)

A plan for new verses, recent revision and long-term revision, matched to your pace.

Arabic reading

Read Arabic text with the right vowels and pronunciation, so you can follow what you recite.

Islamic basics

Prayer, short duas, the life of the Prophet ﷺ and everyday Fiqh, at a level that suits you.

Questions

What age can children start?

From about 5 years old. Lessons for young children are short and gentle.

Do I need anything special?

A phone, tablet or computer with a camera, a stable internet connection and a mushaf.

I cannot read Arabic yet. Can I still learn?

Yes. We start from the letters and go at your pace. Adult beginners are very welcome.

Can I learn in person?

Yes, in Zagazig, Egypt. Message me to arrange it.

How do I pay?

We agree on payment after the trial lesson. Please never send payment details through this website.
